How these statistics are collected

Free Game Deals scans the Steam store, the Epic Games Store and the GOG catalog for games that temporarily drop to 100% off. Each confirmed offer is stored in the deal history and announced by the Discord bot. “Games Found” is the total number of deals tracked, and “Active Servers” is the number of Discord servers currently configured to receive alerts.
